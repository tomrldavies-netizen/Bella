Getting the app on their phone

iPhone 1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen
3. Open it from the Home Screen and allow alerts
Android 1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Tap Install app (or menu, Add to Home screen)
3. Allow alerts
